Career Advice

Tips to help you prepare for your dream career.

1. Build a Professional Resume

Highlight your education, technical skills, projects and certifications. Keep your resume clear, professional and relevant to the job.

2. Improve Your Technical Skills

Practise Python, SQL, HTML, CSS and other skills relevant to your chosen career. Build projects to demonstrate your knowledge.

3. Prepare for Interviews

Practise common interview questions, explain your projects clearly and improve your communication skills.

4. Create a Strong Portfolio

Add your projects to GitHub and keep your professional profiles updated to showcase your work to recruiters.

5. Apply Consistently

Search for relevant openings, customise your resume for each role and track your applications.
